## South African Coal Quality Lab Analytics & Export Grading 🧪🇿🇦

### Business Problem:
A coal processing plant in Mpumalanga needs to automatically grade core samples from incoming mine seams to ensure compliance with export market contracts vs domestic Eskom baseload grid distribution standards. 

### Core Skills Demonstrated:
* Data Quality Profiling & Automation (`CASE WHEN` logic)
* Hard-rock laboratory evaluation framework simulation
* Multi-variable threshold logic mapping

### Industrial Grading Standards Applied:
* **Premium Export (RBCT):** Ash $\leq$ 16.0% AND CV $\geq$ 26.0 MJ/kg
* **Domestic Power (Eskom):** Ash $\leq$ 30.0% AND CV $\geq$ 20.0 MJ/kg
* **Discard / Reject:** Ash > 30% or underperforming energy metrics

### Lab Findings:
* **Seam 2** consistently yields highly marketable **Premium Export (RBCT)** material with optimal ash content profiles (~14.5% - 15.2%).
* **Seam 4** displays elevated washability risks, with sample `SMP-004` triggering **Discard / Reject** protocols due to a high ash volume of **32.4%**.
